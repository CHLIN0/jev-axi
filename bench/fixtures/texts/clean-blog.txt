Why I switched from tabs to spaces (and back)

For ten years I was a tabs person. Then a new team, a new linter, and a
week of merge conflicts later, I gave in. Spaces it was. Two years after
that I found myself on a Go codebase and, well, gofmt has opinions.

The lesson is not about whitespace. It is that the cost of a convention is
almost always lower than the cost of arguing about it.
